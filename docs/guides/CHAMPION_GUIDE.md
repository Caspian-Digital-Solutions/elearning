# Champion guide

## Your role

Champions **double-mark** a sample of assignment submissions to check marking quality. You remain a learner for your own training.

## What you can do

- Open **Marking** and work on submissions **assigned to you** for double-mark.
- See your own progress and marks on your work.

## Prompt library (Content › Prompts)

- Draft prompts for **your department** and **Submit for review**; you cannot mark prompts as tested or import JSON.

## Skills catalogue (Content › Skills)

- Edit **draft** certification Skills for **your department**: catalogue fields, paste the instructions file, record test cases, and **submit for review**.
- You cannot approve Skills (that is the department manager and a trainer) or set a Skill live.

## What you cannot do

- See other learners’ marks or Week 0 totals.
- Change roles, settings, or anyone else’s plan.
- Access the audit log or admin areas.

## Tips

- Complete double-marks promptly so trainers can release feedback.
- If you are locked out, contact Mahgol or Bharg.

# Learner quick start

## Sign in

Use your **CDS Google account** (`@caspiands.com`). If you see “account almost ready”, wait for a trainer to assign your department.

## Week 0

Complete the Week 0 assessment and confirm the **data rules** help page when prompted.

## My path

After a trainer confirms your stream, **My path** shows this week, overdue, bridges, test-outs, and done modules.

## Lessons and assessments

- **Lessons:** mark complete as you go.
- **Assessments / assignments:** follow instructions; confirm the data declaration on hand-in.

## Test-outs

Some modules stay **required** until you pass a test-out quiz or assignment.

## Privacy

Read the **privacy notice** under Help when asked. Ask Bharg or the governance owner if you have questions about your data.
